Officium

You sit at the scribe's desk. Citizens queue up with Latin to translate, forms to choose, words to parse and sentences to unscramble. Help them before they lose patience.

Officina

A top-down workshop for 1 to 4 scribes. Take a wax tablet, collect the right Latin words from the shelves, seal it and hand it to the messenger before the clock runs out.
