Sur cet appareil

One or two chefs sharing this screen and keyboard.

Chef 1: WASD to move, Space or E to grab and drop.
Chef 2: arrow keys, Enter. Touch screens get a joystick.

Partie de classe

The whole class plays at once. Pupils join at the Ludus join page with a code, as for a quiz.
